Reinstall from your phone

After a dealer update the launcher may be gone. This page says how to put it back without a computer, and what this phone can do.

First, look

If the launcher is still on the car after the update, there is nothing to do.

1 · Android phone + cable + Chrome

Likely — to confirm on the car

You need

  • ADB switched on in the car's engineering menu.
  • A USB-C OTG adapter for the phone.
  • The USB-A to USB-A data cable.
  • Chrome on the phone, and the launcher's file.

Steps

  1. Car in P. Switch on ADB.
  2. Adapter, cable, car.
  3. Open the installer, Connect, Allow.
  4. Install the file.
  5. Plug the stick into the car and bring your setup back.
